Offshore Services

Time-charter, spot charter and bareboat charter—Seaspeed has deep expertise chartering offshore vessels. With several Sale and Purchase deals successfully delivered, our team of experts can provide advice on a broad range, from new builds to end-of-life.


Contact Details: offshore.auh@seaspeedmarine.com

Here is a list of all the vessels we provide:

AHTS & AHT

Designed to perform Anchor Handling and towing services. Our vessels also function as Straight Supply Vessel (“SSV”) and can serve as safety standby rescue and fire fighting vessels for oil spill response and recovery efforts.

Platform Supply Vessel

Our platform supply vessel range includes smaller to medium-sized PSV vessels and larger multipurpose PSVs. These vessels are specialized for efficient supply and support operations worldwide.

Tugboats

ASD Tugs, Harbour Tugs, Twin Screw, Tow Tugs, Ocean Tugs - we have a vast network of Tugs able to supply vessels from 10T Bollard Pull to 90T Bollard Pull for Rig towing operations.

Crewboats

Designed to transport crew, supplies and equipment - our vessels range from 10 pax to 100 pax depending on project needs.

Barges

Designed to carry heavy bulk, such as coal, sand, or oil. It provides large and efficient transportation, delivering massive amounts in a single trip.

Landing Crafts

Designed to transport cargo, vehicles, and equipment directly to shorelines, often for offshore oil & gas support.

What kind of vessel support can Seaspeed provide?
