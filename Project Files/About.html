Transforming Maritime Projects, Delivering Memorable Results.

Founded in 2007 and based in the UAE, Seaspeed has been a trusted leader in the shipping industry, serving clients across all sectors. Our mission is to provide exceptional value by offering customized solutions that streamline shipping projects and maximize benefits for our clients.

We pride ourselves on our highly professional and dedicated team, which works closely with clients to understand their specific needs. By delivering vessels that precisely align with project requirements, we ensure that each project is executed efficiently and effectively.

At Seaspeed, we don’t just deliver vessels—we are with you every step of the way, providing ongoing support throughout the entire project lifecycle. Our goal is to help you achieve success by offering comprehensive, tailored solutions that meet the highest standards of quality and performance.

Let Seaspeed handle your journey to excellence, alongside our current amount of 67,000 tonnage under our advisory, 400+ Vessels serviced, and a large network of Partner owner/operators across the Globe. Partner with Seaspeed and stay ahead of the competition with market intelligence and leveraged expertise.

We are trusted by:

The Team:

Our team is composed of seasoned experts, including Master Mariners, Chief Engineers, and professionals with strong commercial acumen. With extensive experience across the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, India, Nigeria, and beyond—covering regions like the North Sea, Malaysia, and Brazilian waters—we understand the unique demands of these markets. We specialize in managing a wide range of vessels, from Bulk Carriers and Oil Tankers to Offshore vessels like AHTS, PSV, and Jack-up Barges. Our expertise spans across both main fleet and offshore operations, ensuring seamless project execution in diverse environments.

Our Leader:

Seaspeed is founded and led by Captain Anil Agarwal, who now serves as our Chief Executive Officer. With over 20 years of comprehensive experience both at sea and offshore, he brings a wealth of expertise to our team. His astute leadership ensures the seamless day-to-day running of our operations, guaranteeing efficiency and excellence in every aspect. In addition to his maritime background, Capt. Anil has a MBA degree, enhancing our team with strategic insight and business acumen. With an unrelenting commitment to excellence and a profound understanding of both operational and commercial dynamics, he plays a pivotal role in driving our company’s success and ensuring our ventures thrive in challenging environments.

QHSE Policy:
