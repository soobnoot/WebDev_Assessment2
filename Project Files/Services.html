Offshore Chartering

We work very closely with owners and charterers, with the endeavour of securing the optimal vessels to meet the charterers' requirements. We deliver comprehensive vessel chartering services tailored to your needs. Bareboat, Time-Charter, Voyage Charter—Seaspeed is adept at tailoring vessel hire terms as per your needs.

Dry Bulk Chartering/Broking

With a team of worldwide experience in the dry bulk services, we hold the latest information about the market and provide guidance for the chartering of bulk carriers. Whether you’re looking to charter a bulk carrier or employ a vessel in the spot market, Seaspeed Marine bulk chartering leads their clients through the process, providing a successful conclusion.

Tanker Chartering/Broking

Our team of tanker brokers work closely with oil trading clients in transporting crude oil, petroleum products, chemicals, and LPG cargo on a worldwide basis, to and from refineries and storage terminals all around the world. Our specialist team deals with tankers of varying sizes – from 3000 dwt vessels transporting refined petroleum products (such as naphtha, jet fuel, gasoline, gas oil, and fuel oil) to 330,000 dwt crude oil tankers. Covering a range of chartering options—Spot Charters, Time Charters, CoA's and Bareboat Charters.

Technical Management

Safe and Efficient Operations Management, Liaison with Owners/Charterers, Planned Maintenance Systems, Regular Condition Assessments, Dry Dock Planning & Supervision and Budget Controls. We cover the full range of technical management services to ensure streamlined and efficient operations, so you can put your focus on revenue growth.

Marine Consultancy

We cater to the full gambit of marine surveys & audit segments and undertake a wide variety of consultancy, surveys, inspections and audits in the offshore shipping and main fleet domain. Inspections are carried out on behalf of shipping companies, cargo underwriters, third parties, and etc.
